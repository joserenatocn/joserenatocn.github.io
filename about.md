# About

My name is Jose Renato, and I'm an aspiring fiction writer. This site is a log of the Bradbury Challenge, my attempt to get better at writing in two ways: reading widely every day, and writing in a form that makes me finish things.

## The challenge

In 2001, Ray Bradbury gave a talk to young writers with two pieces of advice.

**Write a short story every week.** Don't start with novels, which take too long. Write short stories, and at the end of a year you'll have fifty-two. Bradbury's bet was that nobody can write fifty-two bad ones in a row.

**Read a story, a poem and an essay every night for a thousand nights.** Fill your head, and pick the essays from every field you can.

I'm doing both. Each week I draft a story, let it rest, and spend the rest of the week revising a piece from a previous week. Each night I read, and log what I read here.

I'll be as strict as life allows. This is meant to be about enjoyment, growth and discovery, not dread. A missed night doesn't end it.

## Where the stories are

Mostly not here. I want to practice submitting to magazines and get used to rejection, and most magazines won't consider a story that has already appeared online. So what you'll find is the record: what each story attempted, and what I learned from it. Now and then I may post one I've decided not to send out.
