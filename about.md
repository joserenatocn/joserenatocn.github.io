# About

Ray Bradbury's advice to young writers was to write a short story every week, on the theory that it's impossible to write fifty-two bad ones in a row.

This site is my log of trying it. Each week I draft a story in a day or two, let it sit, and spend the rest of the week revising, usually with one craft focus borrowed from Ursula K. Le Guin's *Steering the Craft*, and one narrator stance practiced in the Writers Studio manner.

The stories themselves mostly aren't posted here, because some of them may go out to magazines. What's here is the record: what I tried, and what I learned.
